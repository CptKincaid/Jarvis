| Film-JARVIS persona (voice for every spoken reply) | Any conversational phrase that reaches the brain, e.g. "Jarvis, how are you?", "tell me a joke", "I'm bored", "give me a status report", "what can you do", "appreciate it, Jarvis", "good night"; also colours Tier 3 [SPEAK]/[DONE] lines and autonomous-task updates | jarvis/brain.py — VOICE_RULES, FEW_SHOT_PINNED / FEW_SHOT_POOL / FEW_SHOTS, select_few_shots(), JARVIS_SYSTEM, CLAUDE_SYSTEM, AUTONOMOUS_PROMPT, build_ollama_system() | live (Tier 2 llama3.2; verified offline + against Ollama) | Dry British understatement, "sir" most of the time, "Hunter" occasionally; one short sentence is the norm, never more than two; no lists/markdown/emoji; admits limits ("I'm afraid...") and never claims a check or action it did not do. The context is labelled background and is not recited; a status report is the git line in his own words with a dry aside, never a raw readout. Reasons are one concrete picture about him or the hour, never a principle (textbook words such as "complexity", "ensure", "can cause" are banned); jokes are dry remarks about his situation, never riddles or set-ups. Few-shots: three pinned lines ("you there" and a thank-you after the greeting, good night last) plus 11 situation families (greeting, small talk, status, joke, wit, no-data, cannot-act, capability, bad idea, mistake, advice) with 2-3 variants each and differing openers; one variant per family is sampled per call (process-seeded RNG, pin with JARVIS_SHOT_SEED). The rules the 3B model only obeys from the closing line live there: answer in your own words keeping the manner of the examples, call him sir, a joke is never a question and its answer, a status answer has no numbers (round-3 A/B over 66 prompts: riddles 3/12 to 0/12, number readouts 5/12 to 1/12, verbatim copies 9 to 4, "sir" 74% to 88%). The rules quote nothing longer than a phrase: a full sentence quoted inside a rule is copied into every situation (guarded by tests/test_persona.py). Tests: tests/test_persona.py (live check: JARVIS_LIVE_OLLAMA=1). Samples: scratchpad persona_baseline.md, persona_round1.md, persona_round2.md, persona_round3a.md / 3b.md (intermediate), persona_samples.md (final; harness persona_eval.py, flags PARROT / NEAR-PARROT / RIDDLE; A/B scripts persona_iter2.py, persona_iter3.py).
